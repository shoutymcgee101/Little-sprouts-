Our Program

✨ Our program includes

A well-rounded day of learning, faith, and play designed for 4- and 5-year-olds.

  • Early Reading & Phonics
  • Math Readiness
  • Science & Discovery
  • Writing & Fine Motor Skills
  • Arts & Crafts
  • Music & Movement
  • Bible Lessons & Character Building
  • Outdoor Play & Exploration
  • Small Class Sizes with Individualized Attention

We believe every child is unique and capable of amazing things. Our goal is to plant the seeds of learning today so your little sprout can blossom into a confident, lifelong learner tomorrow.
